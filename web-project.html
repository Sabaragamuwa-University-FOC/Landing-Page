SE4106

Web Systems and Technologies

A compulsory group project module for Bachelor of Science Honours Degree Programme in Software Engineering.

Overview

This course enables students to carry out project work to implement a solution for real-world IT problems, under the supervision of an internal supervisor. The Internal Supervisor provided by the respective department is responsible for helping students with any internal matters and monitoring team interaction.

Internal Supervisors can schedule meetings with students to assess progress and discuss project work throughout Semester IV.

A student is responsible for updating the progress of the project, addressing the feedback given by internal supervisors throughout the semester, and completing the final software product before the end of the semester by covering all requirements and the initially defined scope.

Projects are rigorously evaluated by external/internal supervisors and the same panel of evaluators in both the Proposal Presentation and the Final Evaluation.

Key Objectives

Offer students a glimpse into real-world problems and challenges that need web technology-based solutions.

Enable students to create very precise specifications of the web-based IT solution to be designed.

Create awareness among students of the characteristics of web-based domain areas where IT can be effectively used.

Improve the team building, communication and management skills of the students.

Helps in exploring career opportunities in the area of web development and gives insight into real organizations.

Develop students' ability to apply acquired knowledge to create real-world software and present/demonstrate their work.
