Repository Guidelines

Standardized naming conventions for all academic projects hosted within the organization.

Project Categories

  • Capstone Projects - Final year comprehensive projects
  • Research Projects - Academic research initiatives
  • Web Projects - Web development coursework
  • Mini Projects - Semester-based minor implementations

The Capstone Naming Formula

Cap _ Dept _ BatchYear _ GrpNumber _ Project-Title

Examples

Cap_SE_20_05_Automated-Grading-System
Cap_DS_21_12_Predictive-Crop-Analytics
Cap_CIS_22_03_Smart-Inventory-Tracker

For other projects, replace Cap with Web, Mini, or Research.
