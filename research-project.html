About the research project

SE8101 is where Software Engineering undergraduates move from building software to investigating it. Instead of a group implementation project, each student takes a single research question, designs a study around it, and defends the result on the evidence they gather.

Projects are carried out individually. A student works with one or more internal supervisors drawn from the Department of Software Engineering and, where the topic calls for it, from Computing & Information Systems or Data Science. Supervisors guide scope, method, and rigour throughout the year; the research question and the work itself remain the student's own.

Every completed project is written up as a structured abstract and collected in a batch abstract book, which the department publishes at the end of the year. Those books are archived below, and each project has its own page with the full abstract, keywords, supervisors, source code and dataset links, and any publications that came out of the work.
